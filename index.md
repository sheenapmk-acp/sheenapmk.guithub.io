## About Me
I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Alphonsa College, Pala.  
My research focuses on **Fractional Differential Equations**, specifically numerical solutions of fractional models arising from neuronal communication processes.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics from **Bharathidasan University** in 2020.  
I have 5 years of teaching experience and have participated in several national workshops, seminars, and faculty development programs.

---

## Research Specialization
- **Fractional Differential Equations**
- Numerical methods for fractional models
- Neuronal communication modeling
